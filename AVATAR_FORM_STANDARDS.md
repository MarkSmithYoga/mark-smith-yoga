# Smile Asana Avatar Form Standards

Use this file when generating or replacing pose images. Do not trace or copy another site's photos.
Create original avatar artwork that follows these form targets.

## Global Style

- Red and black Smile Asana palette.
- Clean single-person avatar.
- Full body visible unless the movement is specifically wrist or hand focused.
- No cropped limbs.
- No floating body parts.
- No extra people.
- No text inside the image.
- Clear mat or floor relationship when the pose is weight-bearing.
- Keep the silhouette readable at small card size.

## Alignment Targets

### Standing Forward Fold

- Feet grounded.
- Hips stacked over heels.
- Torso folds toward thighs.
- Knees can be softly bent.
- Neck relaxed.
- Arms reach toward floor, shins, or feet.

### Half Lift

- Feet grounded.
- Spine long and roughly parallel to floor.
- Hands on shins, thighs, blocks, or floor.
- Chest reaches forward.
- Neck long, not dropped.

### Low Lunge

- Front knee bends over the front foot.
- Back leg reaches long or back knee is down.
- Hips face forward.
- Chest stays lifted.
- Hands can be on floor, blocks, thigh, or overhead.

### Down Dog

- Hands and feet grounded.
- Hips high.
- Spine long.
- Knees can bend.
- Shoulders externally stable, not collapsed.

### Plank / Low Plank

- Hands under or slightly ahead of shoulders.
- Body line long from head to heels.
- Elbows organized close enough to support shoulders.
- Knees-down option is acceptable if clearly shown.

### Cobra / Up Dog

- Chest reaches forward.
- Shoulders pull away from ears.
- Legs extend behind.
- Backbend is spread through the spine, not only low back.

### Crow / Arm Balance

- Hands grounded with visible palm contact.
- Weight shifts forward.
- Knees connect to upper arms.
- Spine rounds or stabilizes depending on the balance.
- Feet lift only if the pose is meant to show the balance.

### Handstand

- Hands grounded.
- Shoulders elevated.
- Ribs contained.
- Hips stacked over shoulders when the drill is a line drill.
- Wall support should be visible for wall drills.
- Avoid banana back unless intentionally showing a backbend handstand.

### Bridge / Wheel

- Feet grounded.
- Knees track forward.
- Chest opens.
- Arms support clearly if hands are on floor.
- Avoid collapsed neck or twisted knees.

### Seated Forward Fold

- Sitting bones grounded.
- Spine folds toward legs.
- Legs extend forward or follow the named variation.
- Knees can bend if it makes the shape realistic.

### Splits / Hamstring Work

- Front leg lengthens.
- Back leg reaches behind or kneels.
- Pelvis stays organized.
- Props/blocks are acceptable and often clearer.

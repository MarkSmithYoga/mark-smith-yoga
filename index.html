Teacher Heart / Carry this into the room

A steadier place to teach from.

Short reflections for yoga teachers who feel doubt, comparison, pressure, or the quiet ache of wanting to serve well. Read one before class and let it soften the nervous system.

Carry this

You do not have to perform being a teacher.

You can be clear. You can be warm. You can be honest about what you know and gentle with what you are still learning. The room does not need a perfect person at the front. It needs a steady human being willing to hold space.
